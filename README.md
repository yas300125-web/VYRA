## 1. Project Overview

RoomStyle AI is an existing AI-powered interior design product that allows users to upload a room image, select a design style, and generate a redesigned version of their space.

Our project, **RESTYLE**, proposes upgrading the existing product into a more intelligent, interactive, and personalized AI Interior Design Platform.

Instead of simply generating a redesigned room image, our vision is to help users customize their spaces, compare design alternatives, estimate costs, discover furniture, and make informed interior design decisions.

Our goal is to transform the experience from:

**"Generate a New Room"**

to:

**"Design Your Room With AI."**

## 2. Project Idea

RESTYLE focuses on improving the existing RoomStyle AI user experience through 15 proposed features combining artificial intelligence, computer vision, personalization, augmented reality, and e-commerce.

The upgraded platform would support users throughout their interior design journey, from inspiration and customization to budgeting, shopping, and visualizing their final space.

Our proposal aims to transform RoomStyle AI from a simple AI Room Generator into a comprehensive AI Interior Design Assistant.

**This project is currently a product upgrade proposal rather than a fully implemented application.**

## 3. Problem Statement

Existing AI room redesign experiences can generate visually appealing results, but users may still face several limitations:

- Limited control over individual furniture and room elements.
- Difficulty preserving existing room features.
- Lack of budget and cost estimation.
- Limited comparison between design alternatives.
- No direct connection between generated designs and purchasable furniture.
- Uncertainty about room measurements and furniture suitability.
- Limited visualization under different lighting conditions.
- Difficulty translating AI-generated inspiration into real-world decisions.

Our proposed upgrade addresses these challenges by creating a more practical, flexible, and personalized interior design experience.

## 4. Proposed Features

### Feature 01 — Multiple Restyles

Generate several design alternatives for the same room, such as Modern, Japandi, and Luxury.

Users would be able to compare different options and select the design that best matches their preferences.

### Feature 02 — Selective Restyle

Allow users to modify specific room elements, such as sofas, walls, or flooring, while preserving the remaining design.

For example:

- Change the sofa.
- Change the wall color.
- Change the flooring.

### Feature 03 — Don't Change (Element Locking)

Enable users to lock selected room elements so the AI preserves them during redesign.

Examples include:

- Sofa
- Flooring
- Windows
- Doors
- Existing furniture

### Feature 04 — Mix Styles

Allow users to combine multiple interior design styles to create more personalized results.

Examples:

- 70% Japandi + 30% Industrial
- Modern + Luxury
- Minimal + Scandinavian

### Feature 05 — Mood-Based Design

Enable users to select their desired atmosphere rather than requiring knowledge of interior design terminology.

Available moods could include:

- Cozy
- Calm
- Warm
- Luxury
- Energetic
- Minimal

The AI would translate the selected mood into suitable colors, lighting, materials, and furniture.

### Feature 06 — Budget Mode

Generate budget-conscious design recommendations based on the user's available budget.

The feature would provide:

- Estimated furniture costs.
- Budget-friendly alternatives.
- Spending priorities.
- Suggested essential items.
- Estimated total design cost.

### Feature 07 — Shop This Look

Connect AI-generated interior designs with relevant furniture recommendations.

Users could select furniture items within a generated design to explore:

- Product type
- Color
- Material
- Estimated dimensions
- Price range
- Similar products
- Affordable alternatives

### Feature 08 — Smart Room Analysis

Use computer vision to identify existing room elements, including:

- Walls
- Floors
- Windows
- Doors
- Sofas
- Beds
- Tables
- Lighting

This feature would support more accurate and selective design modifications.

### Feature 09 — Smart Measurements

Estimate room and furniture dimensions from uploaded images to improve furniture recommendations and spatial suitability.

Measurements would remain approximate unless verified by the user.

### Feature 10 — Day & Night Preview

Allow users to preview the same interior design under different lighting conditions:

- Morning
- Afternoon
- Sunset
- Night

This would help users understand how colors, lighting, and materials may appear throughout the day.

### Feature 11 — AR Furniture Preview

Allow users to visualize selected furniture inside their actual rooms using augmented reality.

The goal is to help users evaluate furniture placement and appearance before purchasing.

### Feature 12 — Design History

Save previously generated designs and allow users to:

- Revisit earlier concepts.
- Compare design versions.
- Continue editing saved designs.
- Save favorite results.

### Feature 13 — AI Design Explanation

Provide understandable explanations of AI-generated design choices.

For example:

"We chose warm lighting to create a cozy atmosphere."

"Neutral colors were selected to make the room feel more spacious."

This would help users understand the reasoning behind design recommendations.

### Feature 14 — Before & After Comparison

Introduce an interactive comparison slider allowing users to compare the original room with the AI-generated design.

**Original Room ↔ AI-Generated Design**

### Feature 15 — AI Transformation Video

Convert before-and-after designs into short transformation videos.

The feature would allow users to visualize the redesign process and share the results on:

- Instagram
- TikTok
- Pinterest

## 5. Proposed User Journey

The upgraded user experience would follow these steps:

1. Upload a room image.
2. AI analyzes the room.
3. Choose a preferred style, mood, and budget.
4. Generate multiple design alternatives.
5. Customize selected room elements.
6. Compare design options.
7. Explore furniture recommendations and estimated costs.
8. Preview selected furniture using AR.
9. Save the final design.
10. Generate and share a transformation video.

**Upload → Analyze → Personalize → Generate → Customize → Compare → Shop → Preview → Save → Share**

## 6. Project Objectives

The main objectives of RESTYLE are to:

- Improve personalization and user control.
- Enhance the practicality of AI-generated interior designs.
- Support budget-aware design decisions.
- Connect design inspiration with real-world furniture shopping.
- Improve engagement through interactive features.
- Provide a more flexible and user-friendly design experience.
- Help users make informed interior design decisions.
- Increase the overall value and usability of the existing RoomStyle AI product.
